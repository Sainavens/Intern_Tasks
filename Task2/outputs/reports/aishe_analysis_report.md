## 9. Conclusions

1. After applying enrolment thresholds, analysis focuses on States/UTs with meaningful Ph.D. and PG cohorts.
2. Enrolment is highly skewed toward a few large States (Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, West Bengal).
3. Ph.D. and PG enrolment are strongly positively correlated; OLS regression confirms PG scale as a key predictor of Ph.D. scale.
4. Gender parity is closer (or female-favourable) at PG level in several States; Ph.D. still shows male tilt in many regions.
5. ANOVA tests whether mean enrolment differs by geographic zone; results should guide region-specific higher-education planning.
